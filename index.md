# Lola Finance

Lola Finance is a private, self-hosted personal finance application run by David De Santis for
their own household. It shows the household's account balances, net worth, spending by month and
category, and investments, including tax-lot tracking and wash-sale checks for tax-loss harvesting.

- **Not a public service.** There are no sign-ups. The app runs on a home server and can only be
  reached by the operator's own devices over a private network with multi-factor login.
- **Bank connections** use [Plaid](https://plaid.com), with the operator's own accounts only.
- **No data is sold or shared**, or used for advertising.
